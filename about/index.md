# About me

I am a cosmologist and Principal Researcher at the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI) in Daejeon, South Korea. I am also a professor at the University of Science and Technology, where I teach and supervise students in astronomy and cosmology.

My research uses observations of the large-scale structure of the Universe to test cosmological models. I work on galaxy and radio continuum surveys, peculiar velocities, the cosmic microwave background, and statistical methods for extracting cosmological information from data. Current projects include clustering measurements with the ASKAP/EMU survey and analyses using DESI data.

Before moving to Korea, I worked at the University of Queensland and the University of Sussex. I completed my PhD at the University of Portsmouth.
